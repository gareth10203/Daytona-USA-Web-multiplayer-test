DAYTONA CENTRAL LINK — EXPERIMENTAL 11
Build: daytona-rev-a-central-experimental-11

Independent remote game-performance reports, based on experiment 10.
All players must use build 11. Upload the ZIP contents to GitHub Pages.
Connections > Export all reports downloads host and participant game histories.
Open a driver > Download performance downloads their individual report.
Collection is automatic in linked gameplay; Show performance overlay is optional.
Up to 300 samples per cabinet, approximately five minutes of collected gameplay.
Measurements received by the host remain available after returning to the lobby.
Small acknowledged batches ride existing one-second pings and retry retained samples.
Reports distinguish device-reported metrics, host observations, coverage and gaps.
Last host room report remains in Experimental settings until the page reloads.
No invitation, connection/session IDs, network addresses or user agent in shared reports.
Native engine / loader identical to experiments 9 and 10; motion recovery is retained.
State transport, disconnect limits, simulation timing, audio and renderer are retained.
See docs/REMOTE_PERFORMANCE.md for format, limits, cost and validation scope.
No ROM, fixtures, test injection, diagnostic engine or build objects included.
